## 1. What “working pipeline” means

Two bars. Do not mix them.

| Bar | Pass condition | Fail condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Pipeline (process)** | 10 Reels posted in 14 days, each Hook → Screens, scoreboard filled the same day, next 5 shot lists exist before Sunday | Miss a slot, invent talking, skip the scoreboard |
| **Reach (proof)** | 4 of last 5 Reels ≥ 10,000 views **and** skip rate ≤ 45% on those 4 | One lucky 80k + four 400-view slides |

A working pipeline can exist at 800 views. That is still a win for process. **Do not turn on selling** until the reach bar is hit. Views without a repeatable slot-fill are not a pipeline.

Account baseline (31 Aug 2026): August interview/job posts live at **300–1,600 plays**. July TNEA utility hit **3k–33k**. The 10k bar is “return to utility-hit range, every week,” not “go viral once.”

## 3. The five templates (this is the pipeline)

Every Reel is one of these. If an idea does not fit, it does not ship.

| ID | Name | Promise | Hook text (on working b-roll) | Screens (always this order) | Length | End CTA (on last screen) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | **Bounce** | A thing they open and leave | The bounce in 4 words | Source crop → you break / catch the artifact | 28–38s | `Comment if this is your repo` |
| T2 | **Workflow** | How that X/Reddit post actually works | The workflow in 4 words | Source crop → IDE / agent / you catching the lie | 32–42s | `Comment which step you skip` |
| T3 | **Interview** | The question the thread is fighting | The question huge | Source crop → you *do* the check on screen | 30–40s | `Comment your answer in one line` |
| T4 | **Project** | What not to build — and the replacement | The fail project name | Source crop → you click a real page | 32–42s | `Comment your one number` |
| T5 | **Signal** | A post on fire → one action | The number huge | Source crop → one action you type or click | 25–34s | `Save if this is your batch` |

Same skeleton every time. Three slots. Do not invent a fourth.

```
[0–2s]  HOOK   phone clip: you working, silent. One text card.
[2–12s] SRC    laptop screen: the Reddit/X post. Scroll the quote.
[12–end] PROOF laptop screen: the tactic happening. Last card = CTA.
```

A second PROOF screen is allowed (two recordings). A third is not. If the take needs more, the script is too fat.

**Hard rules**

1. Face is in the first 2 seconds as **working b-roll**. You do not talk. You do not look at the lens.
2. Hard cut at 2.0s into the source screen. Same cut every Reel.
3. One idea. Text is the script. Max 6 words per card. Middle third of the frame.
4. Give the full tactic on the PROOF screen. We are not teasing a paid file.
5. Audio = one quiet CapCut “no copyright” bed, same track all week. No voice. No other creator’s original audio. No TTS until 10k is proven (TTS is a second job).
6. Caption is SEO + the same CTA as the last card. 3–5 hashtags max.
7. If you explain a bug, the answer lives on the PROOF screen, not as a spoiled first caption line.
8. If a script cannot be filmed as Hook → Source → Proof, rewrite the script. Do not add talking.

## 5. Filming SOP — Hook → Screens only

This is the whole factory. If a step is not here, skip it.

**Once (Sunday, 3 minutes) — the hook tape**

- Phone on books, side or over-shoulder. Window light. Same hoodie all week.
- You type. You do not talk. You do not look at the camera.
- Record **15 seconds**. That one clip is the hook for all 5 Reels. Different text cards, same tape.
- Do not reshoot because your face looks “off.” Working is the point.

**Then (Sunday, 20 minutes) — laptop screen record**

- 9:16: record the browser/IDE window, or record 16:9 and crop center in CapCut.
- Hide bookmarks. Big font. Dark theme. No Slack / WhatsApp popups.
- For each Reel, two files only: `rN-source.mp4` and `rN-proof.mp4`.
- Source: open the permalink, scroll the quoted lines, 12–20s raw (we use ~8s).
- Proof: do the tactic once, ugly is fine, 20–40s raw (we use ~18s).
- If the proof needs a second window, that is `rN-proof-b.mp4`. Stop at two.

**Edit (CapCut, 15 minutes for all five)**

Save a project called `akay-h2s`. Duplicate it. Do not build a new timeline.

1. Track 1: hook tape 0:00–0:02. Text card from the shot list.
2. Track 2: source 0:02–0:10. Hard cut. Text card on the quote.
3. Track 3: proof 0:10–end. Text cards every 2–3s. Last card = CTA.
4. Same font, same yellow/white, same quiet bed, all week.
5. First frame must read with sound off. Last frame ≈ first hook card (loop).
6. No logo. No “hey guys.” No zoom spam. One hard cut.

**Time budget:** 40 min Sunday for 5 Reels after the first week. If one Reel takes >12 min, the shot list was too fat. Cut a screen, do not add talking.

**What we will not do (effort kill list)**

- Talk to camera. Learn a script. Light a second setup.
- Voiceover. CapCut TTS. Green screen. Talking-head inserts mid-Reel.
- Film a new hook for every Reel.
- Open a tool you do not already have installed.

## 7. Review loop (the factory)

Every Sunday, 20 minutes, before you write the next 5:

1. Open scoreboard. Sort by views.
2. **Keep** the template that hit highest views *and* lowest skip.
3. **Rewrite only the hook card** of any Reel that skipped >50% and still keep the same screens.
4. **Kill** any topic that got <20 comments and <10 saves — even if you liked it.
5. Write next week’s 5 shot lists before you close the laptop.

One variable per week. Week 1 we test the five templates *inside Hook → Screens*. Week 2 we only change hook cards on the weakest two. We do not add talking. We do not add a sixth format.
